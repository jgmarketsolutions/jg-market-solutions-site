Common Questions

The fine print, in plain words

Is there a setup fee?

No. Ad account setup and pixel installation are included in the monthly fee, even when everything has to be built from scratch.

Is there a contract?

No. Month-to-month means exactly that — cancel anytime. The 3- and 6-month options exist only as a discount for paying upfront.

Who pays for the ads themselves?

You do, directly to your own ad accounts — the budget never passes through JG Market Solutions. How much to spend gets discussed up front before anything launches, and you can see every dollar in your own account at any time.

Does my business need to be local to work together?

No — clients are supported wherever they're located. Video calls and email make distance a non-issue.

What's the percentage-of-sales option?

For ecommerce and other businesses where sales can be tracked directly through the pixel, a percentage of sales is sometimes an option instead of the flat fee — but only once the ads are proven to be worth more than the flat fee. It gets discussed case by case.
